## Info from Stripe

I tried to contact Stripe sales, but they have gated their sales team and non-profit discount info with a business email (not gmail.com) or creating an account, which requires linking a bank account.

This is their generic/standard pricing: https://stripe.com/pricing#standard-pricing.

- pay via credit card: 2.9% + 30¢ 
- pay via debit card: 0.8%

They have discounts for non-profits if you are only taking 'donations'. The discount isn't available for "memberships". However, I looked at the IRS rules and they say donations are 100% deductible if the donator is 'getting nothing in return', like a hat or t-shit. Even then, if the donation is $25 and the t-shirt is a $5 value, then then $20 is tax deductible. We don't give anything in return so we could just call our membership a 'donation' instead.

- Discounts for non-profits: https://support.stripe.com/questions/fee-discount-for-nonprofit-organizations
